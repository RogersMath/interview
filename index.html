Professional summary

Instructional designer and learning center leader with twenty years at Palm Beach State College. I design and build learning objects, asynchronous courses, and faculty training, and I consult directly with faculty on course design and instructional technology.

My work is grounded in learning science and released under open licenses so the college can reuse and adapt it. Recent projects include two asynchronous Canvas courses built to CRLA tutor certification standards and a suite of openly licensed math learning tools now in use by faculty across the mathematics department.
